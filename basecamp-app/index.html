Private Airdrop Claim

Compute your leaf hash offline. Your account ID never leaves this page.

1 — Compute leaf hash


    
Give the distributor this leaf hash to include in the Merkle tree. Your account ID stays private.

  


  

    
2 — Generate claim proof

    
Once the distributor publishes the Merkle root, use the CLI to generate your proof:

    
airdrop-claim prove \
  --account-id <your-id> \
  --allocation <N> \
  --distributor-id <dist-id> \
  --merkle-root <root> \
  --leaf-index <N> \
  --merkle-path <sibling,sibling,...> \
  --recipient-note <hex> \
  --out claim-receipt.bin

    
The proof is generated locally. Your account ID is a private input and does not appear in the output.

  


  

    
3 — Submit claim

    
Submit claim-receipt.bin to the on-chain claim instruction with your recipient note. The on-chain program verifies the receipt and marks your nullifier spent.
